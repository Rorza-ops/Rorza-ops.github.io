		Task pre-empting the Low-Priority (LP) Task:
			Medium-Priority (MP) Task pre-empts the LP Task while the MP Task holds the semaphore.
			Reason:
				1). LP takes the semaphore first with no delays (tskIDLE_PRIORITY + 1)
				2). While LP task is in critical section, the MP task becomes ready after 100ms delay.
				3). Since MP task has higher priority, it pre-empts the LP task, even though the LP task has the semaphore.
				4). MP task delays the LP task completion of its critical section, thus priority inversion.
